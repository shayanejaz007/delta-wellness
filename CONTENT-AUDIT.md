## Schema decisions

- `Organization` and `Person` used.
- `ScholarlyArticle` used on publication pages, with only fields present in the papers.
- `MedicalOrganization`, `MedicalClinic` and `Physician` **not used** — no supplied
  document establishes those classifications.
- No structured data asserts anything not visible on the page.

## Standing rules

1. No claim ships without an entry in `lib/sources.ts` or this file.
2. Hedged wording in a source stays hedged. "May" never becomes "does".
3. Publication types are stated as the publisher classified them. A perspective
   article is not described as a peer-reviewed study; a conference presentation
   is not described as a paper.
4. No regulatory language of any kind until a public record is verified.
5. No third party's name, title or affiliation is published on secondary evidence.
